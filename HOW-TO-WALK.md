# Cheryle’s Hikes: walking real mountain hikes on the treadmill

## Going for a walk
1. Open **Cheryle’s Hikes** and tap a hike, for example *Skyline Trail, Mount Rainier*.
2. Under **Walk for**, pick how long your break is (10–30 minutes, or the whole hike).
3. Stand on the side rails and tap **Start walking**. After 3‑2‑1 the belt starts slowly. Step on.
4. Walk. When the hiker in the video climbs, the treadmill tilts up. On steep parts it slows a little, and the video speeds up or slows down to match your steps.
5. When your time is up, there's a 30‑second cool‑down and the belt stops by itself. Next time, the hike card says **Continue**, and the hike picks up where you left off.

- **Need to stop?** Tap the big red **PAUSE** (or press the space bar). The belt and the video both stop. Tap **RESUME** to carry on.
- **Too fast or too slow?** Tap **− slower** or **+ faster**.
- **Want to finish early?** Tap **End walk**. Your place is saved.
- **On the TV:** turn the phone sideways and use Screen Mirroring to the TV.
- **Safety:** the treadmill's own remote and stop button always work. **My limits** on the main screen caps your top speed and steepest tilt.

## Adding a new hike
1. On YouTube, find a full hike filmed while walking, with a steady view and no talking. Searching "*trail name* hike POV" or "virtual hike" works well. Copy its link.
2. In Cheryle’s Hikes, tap **+ Add a hike from YouTube** and paste the link.
3. Type the **trail name** (just the main words, like "Skyline Trail") and the **park or town** nearby, then tap **Find trail**. Tap the result whose length matches the video. Video titles often give the miles.
4. Check the direction. The video's first minutes should match the start of the hill picture: if the hike starts by climbing, the picture should rise on the left. If it doesn't, tap **⇄ Walk it the other way**.
5. Skip any intro in the video, then tap **Use video time** at the moment the hike starts. Do the same at the end.
6. Tap **Save this hike**.

**Tip:** tap **Save a backup of my hikes** now and then. If the phone is ever reset, **Load a backup** brings them all back.
