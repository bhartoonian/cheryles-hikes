How it works

  1. Pick a hike and how long you want to walk, then tap Start walking.
  2. The video starts, and after a 3‑2‑1 countdown the belt starts slowly. The first 30 seconds are a warm‑up.
  3. When the hiker in the video climbs, the treadmill tilts up. On steep parts it slows a little, and the video speeds up or slows down to match your steps.
  4. PAUSE (the big red button, or the space bar) stops the belt and the video. Tap it again to carry on.
  5. When your time is up you get a 30‑second cool‑down, then the belt stops. Next time the hike picks up where you left off.
  6. The treadmill's own remote and stop button always work too.

Adding a hike

  1. On YouTube, find a walking video filmed on a named trail with a steady view and no talking (for example "Angels Landing POV hike"). Copy its link.
  2. Tap + Add a hike, paste the link, then type the trail's name and the park or town it's in, and tap Find trail.
  3. Pick the right trail, line up where the hike starts and ends in the video, and save.

Hikes are saved on this phone. Tap "Save a backup of my hikes" now and then so they're never lost.
